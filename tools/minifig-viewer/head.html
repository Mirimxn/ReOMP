Minifig Part Viewer

Standard minifig body from SUPER_MINIFIG_DX11.GHG. Loaded with the 212th Airborne Clone Trooper as an example. Drop a character's .prefab_baked to make a new character from it.

Drop textures (.texture, .dds, .png) or models (.gsc, .ghg) anywhere. A texture goes to the part its file name names (FRONT, ARM LEFT, HIP, HAT, SATCHEL…), and _NRM, _BRDF or _EMISSIVE in the name picks the map. Click a name to open its material and choose each texture's UV set.
